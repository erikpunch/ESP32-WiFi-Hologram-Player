USB INSTALLER

Flash your projector

  1. Plug the CYD into your computer with a USB data cable.
  2. Click the button below and select its serial port.
  3. Confirm installation and keep the board connected until it finishes.

Installing replaces the program on the connected ESP32. Check the board and display before continuing.

At the workbench

No serial port appears

Try another USB data cable, close Arduino Serial Monitor, and reconnect. If you opened the installer in a preview, open its URL in a separate browser tab. If the port opens but the board cannot synchronize, hold BOOT while connecting and release it when writing begins.

Load your first animation

Download the spinning cube test animation and copy it to the root of a FAT32 microSD card. For your own loops, put a multi-frame .rgb or .rgbv file in the root of the microSD card. Use 320 × 240, RGB565 little-endian, 10 fps, with no file header. Insert the card and restart. Tap the display to advance.

Transfer animations over Wi-Fi

Start the player with at least one valid animation on the card. Once playback begins, join HologramPlayer using password hologram. Open http://192.168.4.1 on that device to manage your files.
